## Concrete compatibility proof — 2026-10-23

Official Bot API: https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#sendpoll and https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#inputpolloption restrict poll question/options to custom emoji entities. A spoiler is therefore sent in ordinary preceding messages, never injected into poll option text. Use exactly A-D labels in the poll; do not use an English/Russian concatenation that breaches poll limits or alters binding.

Ordinary message entities include spoiler and expandable_blockquote; Telegram offsets/lengths use UTF-16. Prefer entity-safe bounded construction over raw HTML interpolation. Complete texts must be retained across chunks, with entity ranges adjusted and no split Unicode surrogate pair or malformed formatting. Disable link previews on source-heavy teaching messages.

Official group delivery: https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#ephemeral-messages-and-commands and https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#ephemeralmessageparameters. Bot administrators may address an ephemeral message to a non-bot member without a callback. A callback-triggered response may include callback_query_id, within15 seconds of that incoming action. Such messages are visible only to that user in the group and delivery is not guaranteed; support explicit recovery. Do not claim durable delivery, client support observation or a successful live send from metadata alone.

Installed grammY1.46.0/@grammyjs/types5.0.0 already declare sendMessage.ephemeral_message_parameters, EphemeralMessageParameters.receiver_user_id/callback_query_id and spoiler/expandable_blockquote. No dependency upgrade, unsafe assertion, raw-token fetch, new provider or separate direct message is required. An ephemeral Message has message_id0 and may have an ephemeral_message_id; never pass it to durable poll/control binders.

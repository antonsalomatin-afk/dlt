## Content and evidence

- `../imports/launch-20261016-car-candidates.json`: 50 original CAR questions.
- `../imports/launch-20261016-motorcycle-candidates.json`: 50 original MOTORCYCLE
  questions.
- `launch-20261016-provenance.json`: per-record primary evidence, page numbers,
  vehicle rationale and reference-bank topic links where an actual topic match
  was identified.
- `launch-20261016-taxonomy.json`: 12 categories and 53 learning concepts with
  stable UUIDs and required labels.
- `launch-20261016-taxonomy.sql`: proposed additive SQL matching that manifest.

The approved [artslob reference](artslob-reference.md), pinned at
`67440927542ae7af7cebe4181c93a2dec07b1259`, was used only to identify topics.
No upstream question wording, choice wording, illustrations or answer keys were
copied into these files. Topics absent from that inventory were developed from
the handbook and are marked `PRIMARY_HANDBOOK_EXPANSION`; empty topic IDs are
intentional. Upstream IDs are discovery links, never independent evidence of
correctness or vehicle applicability.

Every proposed answer and explanation was checked against rendered pages of
the Department of Land Transport's
[Driving in Thailand: Thailand Driving Guide](https://safedrivedlt.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Driving-EN-010468_compressed.pdf).
The manifest pins the PDF SHA-256 and records both its printed and physical PDF
page numbers (physical numbering is 1-based; cited physical pages are printed
pages plus six). Reviewers should inspect the complete cited pages, including
headings and qualifications, rather than infer evidence from the bank's keys.
The handbook cover names the Department of Land Transport, Ministry of
Transport. Its diagrams and text are not redistributed here.

These newly written scenarios cover safety, road positioning, controls,
visibility, vulnerable road users, parking, driver fitness and vehicle readiness.
The motorcycle set additionally covers helmets and road-surface hazards. Shared
rules receive distinct car/motorcycle scenarios and an explicit applicability
rationale. Following-distance variants share one concept; truck-blind-spot,
condition-adjusted speed, pedestrian-yield and breakdown-warning variants are
also grouped by their underlying rule. Unrelated rules are not grouped into a
single general safety concept.

This is a small learning set, **not an official DLT examination bank, a complete
syllabus or a claim of examination readiness**. The target of 50 questions per
vehicle is the application's minimum exam-count boundary, not proof of syllabus
coverage. Numerical legal limits, fines, licensing-equivalence questions,
clinical treatment and disputed answer-key material have been excluded.

All questions, choices and explanations are English only. Thai, Russian and
exam-style English fields are null. The current taxonomy schema requires
non-null Thai and Russian names, so taxonomy labels deliberately repeat English
as a fallback; these are not verified translations. No paid provider or runtime
translation is required.

## Safe additive taxonomy release

After Supervisor release-chain acceptance, the Admin should:

1. Back up the database and record the release SHA. Review the JSON and SQL
   together before applying either. Rehearse in a disposable database with the
   same schema first.
2. Inspect the intended category/concept slugs and UUIDs through a trusted
   database session. The manifest's deterministic UUIDs must be used. Do not
   remap to a different pre-existing identity silently.
3. Run the SQL through a trusted database client with stop-on-error behavior
   and credentials supplied by the host's secret environment. Never put a token,
   password, full DATABASE_URL or credential-bearing connection string into a
   command line, transcript, repository file or report.
4. The SQL runs within one explicit transaction, inserts only absent taxonomy
   rows, and uses `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`. It then asserts every intended
   slug/id/name tuple and category sort order. Any conflicting UUID, slug,
   name or sort order raises an exception and rolls the transaction back.
   Exact replay is safe. Existing unrelated rows and learner state are preserved.
5. If an assertion fails, stop and report the conflicting taxonomy identity
   without learner data or credentials. Diagnose in a new review cycle; do not
   delete, rename or overwrite a production taxonomy row to force this release.

The SQL has no deletes, updates, schema changes, extensions or question-state
changes. Its only purpose is adding exact required categories and concepts.
No broad Prisma seed or development fixture import belongs in this release.

## Recovery and execution evidence

Each batch command is transactional. Stop on an error, inspect both read-only
status reports, and resolve the exact failed stage; do not rerun the whole chain
blindly. An inactive exact DRAFT may be reimported; an inactive exact reviewed
batch may replay its same review; an exact active VERIFIED batch may replay its
activation. **Do not rerun import after review, or import/review after activation**:
those earlier-stage commands intentionally reject later lifecycle states.
If one vehicle completed a stage and the other did not, advance/replay only the
appropriate unchanged stage for each. Never edit IDs, content, keys, review
identity or timestamps to bypass a collision or drift error. Never overwrite
existing taxonomy or silently replace a final decision. Keep service maintenance
in place until both final statuses pass.

Restoring the pre-release backup is an Admin-coordinated destructive recovery,
not an automatic action in these files. If rollback is necessary after learner
activity, stop and diagnose preservation of new learner state before restoring;
do not delete questions or learner records ad hoc.

Record the accepted release SHA, actual UTC execution times, taxonomy/import/
review/activation summaries, final statuses and both vehicle exam results in
`.agent/admin/TASK-071-report.md`, without secrets or learner data. Manifest
issuance times are not substitutes for those execution times. Existing
development fixtures remain separate; this is the approved starter learning set,
not a claim of full official examination coverage.
